# Velanthas

> _"They built it near the wound. Not to mourn it. To prove it hadn't finished them."_

---

## Overview

Velanthas is the relay the surviving [[The Vael People.md|Vael]] built near the ruins of [[Vaelos.md]] — the home [[The Orokin.md|[Saya]].

It is not the home they lost. It was never meant to be. It is the home they built with the hands they had left, near the place they came from, because the alternative was to drift so far from that place that it would stop mattering.

[[Saya]] chose the location deliberately.

---

## Construction

Velanthas was the first act of reconstruction — begun before the Vael had fully stabilized, before the shape of what they were rebuilding was clear. [[Sorin]] was among the first group. Building it was the first thing they did as a people with somewhere to be.

It is Vael-constructed throughout. There is no Orokin infrastructure here — not repurposed, not reclaimed, not present at all. [[Saya]] was specific about this. The gold she wears is reclamation. The home she builds is not built from their ruins.

The materials are practical, the construction functional. Over time it has been made warm — not through grand design but through use, through small things accumulated in rooms, through the specific way a place absorbs the people who live in it.

The colors throughout are black and deep crimson. The colors of the Vael. The colors the sisters carry on their bodies into every room they stand in.

---

## Location

Velanthas sits near [[Vaelos.md]] — close enough that the ancient Void rift running through the planet is a constant low presence in the relay's environment. The [[The Between.md|Between]] hums through the walls here in a way that is quieter than standing on the surface would be, but present enough that Vael-touched people feel it as familiar.

This proximity serves a practical purpose: the resonance of the rift creates a natural interference that makes the relay significantly harder to locate by outside factions. Void cloaking is not perfect, but it is consistent, and Velanthas has remained undetected by those who would find its existence inconvenient.

[[Saya]] cited the practical reason when she chose the location.

She has not spoken about the other reason.

---

## What It Feels Like

Velanthas is functional first. The corridors are wide enough for purpose, the rooms sized for what they contain. It does not try to be beautiful in the Orokin sense — no vaulted ceilings, no gold filigree on the architecture, no aesthetic of power for its own sake.

But it has become, over time, something that feels lived-in. There are spaces where people gather that have acquired warmth without being designed for it. There are small things on walls and surfaces that belong to specific people and have been there long enough to seem permanent.

It feels like something that survived and is still surviving and has decided to keep going.

The royal section carries a different quality — more formal, more deliberate, but not cold. [[Saya]]'s presence in it has made sure of that.

---

## Key Spaces

### The Royal Section

Where [[Saya]] rules from and lives. Formal enough to function as the seat of what remains of Vael leadership. Made warm enough to be habitable by a person who is also a sister and not only an empress.

[[Kael]] has her own room here. Not the room of a guest — the room of someone who belongs, even when she is absent.

### The Warframe Room

[[Kael]]'s collection has its own space in the royal section — large enough to house the frames properly, maintained with the particular care she gives to things she considers kindred. She does the maintenance herself.

This room is hers entirely. [[Saya]] does not enter it without reason. [[Sorin]] stands outside it.

### The Barracks

Where [[Sorin]] and the royal guard are stationed. Functional, spare, exactly what it needs to be. [[Sorin]]'s quarters are near the corridor that connects to the royal section — close enough that the distance between his post and [[Kael]]'s room can be covered quickly if needed.

### The Outer Corridors

The parts of Velanthas that have not yet been fully made warm. Functional, lit, but still carrying something of the relay's original character — the particular quality of a space that was built for use and has not yet been loved enough to lose it.

There are people working on this. It takes time.

---

## Vaelos

Vaelos is visible from certain vantage points on Velanthas — a shape in the distance, scarred and quiet. No one has been to the surface since the fall.

A return is being planned.

The relay sits near the wound deliberately. [[Saya]] has said, once, that a people who cannot look at what was done to them cannot govern themselves in the full weight of what that means. Whether she has looked yet herself is a question she has not answered.

---

## The Name

Velanthas has no direct translation — it is a Vael word that carries the sense of _what was made from what remained._ The naming was informal, collective, the kind of thing that happens when enough people use a word long enough that it stops being a description and becomes a place.

[[Saya]] did not name it. Nobody named it officially. It named itself, the way places do when people love them enough.
