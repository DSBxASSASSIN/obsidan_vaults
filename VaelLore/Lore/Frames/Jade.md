> _"Not every frame asks something of her. Some simply let her be present."_

## Overview

Jade is a Warframe defined by grace under cost — something made beautiful not despite what it has endured but alongside it.

[Kael](https://claude.ai/chat/Kael.md) did not expect to find this.

She finds she is glad she did.

---

## Nature

Jade does not operate through force.

It operates through flow.

It responds to:

- movement with intention
- presence rather than pressure
- the space between strikes

Its design implies something refined. Something that has passed through difficulty and chosen not to let it make it hard.

---

## First Alignment

The facility had been quiet for a long time.

Jade was not in a combat vault. Not in a weapons bay. It had been placed — or had come to rest — in a room with no clear tactical purpose.

Kael had not expected that.

She stood in the doorway and felt the pressure of the space inside — not dormant, not waiting, but _settled_. Something that had made peace with where it was.

She had not felt that in any of the other facilities.

She stepped inside carefully.

Not because she was uncertain.

Because it felt like the right way to enter.

---

## The Bond

The connection with Jade is unlike any of her other frames.

There is no resistance phase.

There is no instability.

There is also no immediacy — no snap of recognition the way there is with Voruna, no weight of shared logic the way there is with Harrow.

There is simply ease.

The connection settles like something that was always going to be comfortable.

Kael does not have many things that feel like that.

---

## Shared Traits

The alignment between them is not built on shared damage.

It is built on something rarer.

Both carry:

- cost that has not made them smaller
- grace that is not performance
- the particular quality of something that has come through the worst of it and chosen, deliberately, how to move afterward

Jade does not ask anything of her.

This is unusual enough to notice.

---

## Behavioral Anomaly

When paired with Kael, Jade exhibits:

- no documented anomalies
- standard Transference parameters
- what would be, in any other frame, unremarkable behavior

The anomaly, if it can be called that, is the absence of strain.

Kael does not return from Jade the way she returns from her other frames.

She returns quieter.

Not emptied.

Settled.

---

## Implication

Kael does not deploy Jade often.

Not because the frame is ill-suited for it.

Because some things are not for the field.

She has not articulated this to anyone.
