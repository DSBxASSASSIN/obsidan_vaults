> _"She does not examine what it costs her. She simply pays it."_

---

## Overview

Harrow is a Warframe built on sacrifice — suffering channeled outward, converted into protection for those nearby.

It does not ask whether the cost is fair.

It simply pays it.

[Kael](https://claude.ai/chat/Kael.md) understands this completely.

---

## Nature

Harrow does not operate through aggression or speed.

It operates through endurance.

It responds to:

- sustained pressure
- proximity of those it protects
- willingness to absorb

Its design assumes that someone must take the damage so others do not have to.

It was built for that assumption.

---

## First Alignment

Harrow did not turn toward Kael when she approached.

It did not move at all.

It was still in the particular way of something that has been waiting — not dormant, but patient. Something that had learned not to expect anything and had settled into the shape of that expectation.

Kael stood in the doorway for a long time before she stepped inside.

She recognized that kind of stillness.

She had worn it herself.

---

## The Bond

The connection with Harrow is not immediate.

There is a resistance phase — not hostility, but something closer to wariness. A frame that has learned to brace rather than open.

Kael did not push through it.

She waited.

When the connection finally took, it settled into something quieter than most of her frames. Less instinct. More weight.

She is careful in Harrow.

Careful in a way that has nothing to do with control.

---

## Shared Traits

The alignment between them is not comfortable.

It is close.

Both operate on:

- sacrifice as reflex rather than choice
- protection as the primary justification for cost
- the habit of absorbing damage before others can reach it

Harrow does not resist her.

But it does not let her forget what she is doing, either.

---

## Behavioral Anomaly

When paired with Kael, Harrow exhibits:

- extended endurance thresholds beyond documented parameters
- no instability at high damage intake
- a quality that operators have described, inconsistently, as _stillness under pressure_

Kael does not document this.

She does not need to.

---

## Implication

Harrow is not the frame she reaches for first.

It is the frame she reaches for when she has already decided that the cost does not matter.

She is quiet for a long time after operating it.

No one asks why.
