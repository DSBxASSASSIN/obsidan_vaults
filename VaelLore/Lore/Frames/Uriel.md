## Overview

Uriel is a Warframe built in the language of light — radiant, structured, carrying the aesthetic of judgment and divine authority.

That language is Orokin.

[[Kael]] does not trust languages she has heard before.

---

## Nature

Uriel does not operate on instinct.

It operates on precision.

It responds to:

- clarity of intent
- discipline of purpose
- the absence of excess

Its design implies something elevated. Something that sees from above and renders verdict.

Kael knows who else built things in that image.

---

## First Alignment

Kael stood outside the chamber longer than usual.

The frame inside was still. Composed. Lit by something internal that had not dimmed despite the years.

She did not approach immediately.

She read the space instead — the pressure of it, the weight of what was inside — and looked for what was underneath the surface.

It took time.

What she found was not what the aesthetics had implied.

The light was real.

But it was not Orokin light.

It was something that had survived being dressed in Orokin clothing and remained, underneath, entirely its own.

She stepped inside.

---

## The Bond

The connection with Uriel is not instinctive.

It is earned.

There is a resistance phase that does not feel like wariness — it feels like _assessment_. A frame that does not give itself to operators who have not demonstrated something worth giving to.

Kael did not take this personally.

She would have done the same.

When the connection took, it was deliberate on both sides. Considered. A recognition that arrived through scrutiny rather than instinct.

She respects it for that.

---

## Shared Traits

The alignment between them is not immediate kinship.

It is arrived-at understanding.

Both operate on:

- judgment earned rather than assumed
- light as something carried rather than performed
- the refusal to be fully defined by what shaped them

Uriel does not respond to Kael the way Voruna does.

But it does not hold back once it has decided.

---

## Behavioral Anomaly

When paired with Kael, Uriel exhibits:

- none of the assessment delay documented with other operators
- what has been described as _sustained coherence_ — a clarity of output that does not degrade under pressure
- a notable absence of the radiance fluctuations observed in other pairings

Kael has not offered an explanation for this.

She is not certain she has one.

---

## Implication

Uriel is the frame that made her reconsider her initial wariness of aesthetics.

What something was built to look like is not what it is.

She knew this already.

Uriel made her feel it.
