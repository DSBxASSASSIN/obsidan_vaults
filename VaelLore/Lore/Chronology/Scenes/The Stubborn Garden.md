# Scene: The Stubborn Garden

## Context

Occurs during the early recovery period following the events of [[The Locked Room.md]]. Set in the small, overgrown interior garden of the relay first mentioned in [[The relay quietly.md]]. Involves:

- [[Kael]]
- [[Saya]]
- [[Sorin]]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Scene

The air in the courtyard was cool. It smelled of damp soil and a sharp, green scent that didn’t belong in a space made of pressurized metal. **Kael** was kneeling by a rusted railing. Her hands moved with the same **methodical precision** she used to repair the gold on her frames—untangling a thick, pale vine that was slowly claiming the walkway. She didn’t follow the [[The Between|Between]]. She followed the roots.

**Saya** sat on a stone bench nearby. She wasn’t wearing her formal regalia. Her hair was down. Between her fingers, she turned the **short-bladed dagger** Kael had given her, feeling the worn handle—the weight of it pulling her back into her body. An anchor. The quiet between them was the comfortable kind.

“Mother used to say these were stubborn,” Saya said. Kael didn’t look up. She snapped a dead twig. The sound was crisp. Real. “It’s winning,” Kael said. “If I don't clear the base, it’ll have the whole railing down by the next watch.” “Don’t,” Saya said softly. “I like it. It just... grows.”

Kael paused, her hand hovering over a leaf. She felt the [[The Between|Between]] pressing at the edges—a faint, sourceless hum—but she pushed it back, focusing on the rough texture of the vine. “It’s a [[Vaelos]] strain,” Kael said. “The one that survives the deep winters”. “I couldn’t remember the name earlier,” Saya admitted. Her fingers found the guard of her dagger. “Talk to me. Tell me something ordinary. What was the name of the man who sold these in the lower markets?”

Kael sat back on her heels. She wiped her hands on her tunic. She thought about a market that didn’t exist anymore. “Vahn,” she said. “He had a limp and a dog that barked at the royal transport every time we passed.” A small, genuine breath of a laugh escaped Saya. “I remember that dog. You told me it was a noble beast doing its duty to protect the street.” “It was,” Kael said simply.

The door mechanism hissed open. **Sorin** stepped into the garden. He moved with his practiced, **unintrusive stillness**. He didn't announce a report. He didn't bring news of a breach. He carried two cups. The steam rose in thin, white curls.

He set one down on the bench beside Saya and handed the other to Kael. “The night is clear,” Sorin said, looking up at the stars. “You can see the [[Vaelos]] silhouette tonight.” Kael accepted the cup. The heat of it grounded her. She looked at the guard—the man who had followed her into [[The Exile|exile]] without being asked. She shifted on the bench. Making a space that wasn't strictly for a guard.

“Stay,” she said. Sorin hesitated for a fraction of a second—a **structural quiet** passing over him—before he moved to the railing. He stood in the circle of their light. The three of them held the quiet together. Two sisters and a man who had survived a corridor he couldn't finish walking. Watching a stubborn plant that refused to stop growing.
