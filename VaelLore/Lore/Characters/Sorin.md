## Overview

Sorin is the royal guard of the surviving Vael — stationed at the relay the sisters built near the ruins of [Vaelos](Vaelos.md), present at the edge of every room [[Kael]] occupies, and defined by a loyalty that is so complete it does not require explanation or acknowledgment.

He does not speak much. He does not need to. He has understood since childhood what it means to stand in a place and not move from it, and he has never found a reason to stop.

---

## History

### Before the Fall

Sorin grew up in a family with a single tradition: the royal guard. His father served the emperor and empress of the Vael — not as a political appointment but as a life's vocation, the particular devotion of someone who has decided that protecting the people who carry a civilization is the most meaningful work available to a person.

His father died at his post when [Vaelos](Vaelos.md) fell. The emperor and empress died with him.

Sorin was in training when it happened. Not yet positioned with the royalty — too junior, rotated elsewhere. He was alive because of where he happened to be standing.

He has never found this a comfort.

He knew [[Kael]] and [[Saya]] by sight before the fall — proximity was unavoidable when training near the royal household — but not by conversation. They were the heirs. He was a candidate. The distance was formal and appropriate and neither of them ever had cause to cross it.

He watched [[Kael]] in those days the way someone watches a person they are training to protect: with attention, without attachment, cataloguing the things that would matter later.

He did not know how much later would cost.

### The Fall

When [Vaelos](Vaelos.md) fell, Sorin's father died at his post. High command was wiped out in the first strike. Sorin watched the princesses being taken and organized what survivors he could find.

He led fourteen men into the Orokin facility to get them back.

He was the only one who came out.

He has never told anyone a rescue was attempted. Not [[Kael]]. Not [[Saya]]. 

→ See [[The Rescue Attempt]] for the full account.

### The Facility

He was captured during the attempt and held in the same facility for the duration of the Orokin's interest in their captives — separated from [[Kael]] and [[Saya]], unable to reach them.

The facility had walls, not silence.

He knew, in outline, that something was being done to them. Not the specifics — not [The Volunteer](The%20Volunteer.md), not the nature of the experiments, not the full weight of what the Orokin were extracting. Just the knowledge that the sounds that came through the walls belonged to someone he had been trained to protect and he could not reach them.

He has never said what he heard.

### After the Fall

When the [Orokin](The%20Orokin.md) collapsed, Sorin found [[Kael]] in the aftermath. He recognized immediately what the months had done to her and said nothing. He presented himself as what he was — a surviving guard candidate, trained, Vael, with nowhere else to be.

She accepted him.

He has been at his post since.

## Personality

Sorin is quiet in the way of someone who learned early that words are for when silence won't serve. He is formal without being cold — there is a warmth underneath the correctness that people who spend time near him eventually notice, though he does nothing to draw attention to it.

He is not humourless. He simply does not perform humour for people who haven't earned it.

He is, in his own accounting, doing his duty. His father did this. He does this. The continuity of it is not something he finds heavy — it is the shape of a life that makes sense to him, in a way that very few shapes have since Vaelos burned. [[Kael]] is alive. [[Saya]] rules. The Vael people have a home near the ruins of the one they lost. He stands at his post.

That is enough.

---

## His Loyalty

Sorin is loyal to the royal line — to [[Kael]] specifically, and to [[Saya]] as empress, in that order. This is not a secret and not a slight. It is simply the architecture of how he understands his duty: [[Kael]] is what he was trained toward, and she is the one who accepted him, and she is the one whose life he is closest to in the daily work of guarding it.

He is respectful to [[Saya]]. Completely, formally, without reservation. But if the two of them were ever in conflict — as they were during the exile — he stands where he stands.

[[Saya]] understands this. She does not entirely disapprove.

During the exile, when [[Saya]] found him posted outside the door of the relay station [[Kael]] had claimed, she told him he was AWOL. He agreed. She told him [[Kael]] was exiled. He agreed.

_Exile doesn't change who she is,_ he said.

She walked past him through the door. He stayed outside.

## What He Carries

Sorin carries three things that no one else knows:

**The mission** — He organized a rescue. People followed him into that facility. None of them came out except him. He has never said their names aloud since. He has not forgotten them.

**The facility** — He was in the same building while [[Kael]] went through the experiments. He heard what he heard. He knows, in outline, what was done to her — not the specifics, not the full weight, but enough that when people speak of the Orokin in abstract terms he feels something specific and does not show it.

**His father** — He watched his father choose this life and give everything to it and die for it when it mattered. He made the same choice. He does not know if that is devotion or inheritance or something he never had the language to distinguish between. He has not spent much time trying to find out.

---

## What [[Kael]] Knows

[[Kael]]'s connection to [The Between](The%20Between.md) reads more than she is given. She knows Sorin is hiding something. She has known since the first moment she looked at him in the aftermath and felt the shape of something contained and old and untouched beneath the surface of his presentation.

She also knows there is no malice in him. Whatever he carries, it is not a threat to her or to [[Saya]].

So she filed it and left it alone.

She has never told him she knows. He has never told her what he knows. Between them this is an unspoken agreement that functions, in its own way, like the agreement between the sisters about [The Volunteer](The%20Volunteer.md) — a thing both people are aware of, that neither pulls at, that is allowed to stay closed because the cost of opening it is higher than the cost of carrying it.

---

## What [[Saya]] Knows

[[Saya]] found his name in the old Vaelos files while doing her due diligence on the people serving in the guard. A promising candidate. Pre-fall records. His family name connected to generations of royal service, his father listed among those who died protecting the emperor and empress.

She has not told him she looked. She has not told [[Kael]].

She does not know about the rescue mission. She does not know about the facility.

But she has noticed, in the way Saya notices things, that his loyalty to [[Kael]] is too specific and too rooted to be purely formal. It has the texture of something personal — something that happened and left a mark — and she has not yet decided whether to pull that thread.

---

## The Relay

Sorin is stationed at the relay the sisters built near [Vaelos](Vaelos.md) — the home the surviving Vael have made in the shadow of the world they lost. He has his duties, his rotations, his formal role in the structure of the guard.

He has not been back to the surface of Vaelos itself.

He has not decided yet whether he will.
