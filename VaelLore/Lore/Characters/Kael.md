## Overview

Kael is the elder of the two sisters and the firstborn of a Vael lineage that, by tradition, carried the responsibility of leadership. By every right of her people she was heir — she had the presence, the training, the natural depth of [Void connection](The%20Between.md) that her teachers spoke of in quiet tones.

She gave the throne to her younger sister [[Saya]] without hesitation and stepped into the shadow to become the blade [[Saya]] would never have to be.

She has never regretted it. She would do it again.

---

## History

### Before the Fall

Kael grew up on [Vaelos](Vaelos.md) in a family with deep ties to Vael leadership tradition. Her connection to [The Between](The%20Between.md) was strong from an early age — not violent or unstable, but _deep_, like a river rather than a rapids. She trained seriously. She took her future responsibilities seriously. She loved her younger sister with the particular ferocity of someone who decided early that protecting her was the most important thing she would ever do.

### The Fall of Vaelos

When [The Orokin](/The%20Orokin.md) came, Kael was nineteen. She fought. It was not enough — nothing was enough against the full weight of an empire. She survived, along with [[Saya]] and a handful of others. They were captured.

→ See [Vaelos](Vaelos.md) for the full account of the fall.

### The Experiments — [The Volunteer](The%20Volunteer.md)

When the [Orokin](/The%20Orokin.md) selected [[Saya]] for Void experimentation, Kael volunteered to take her place.

She said it simply. As if the decision had already been made.

She came back from the first session with burns she wrapped before her sister could see. She came back from later sessions with scars, and silences, and something behind her eyes that hadn't been there before. Each time worse. Each time saying nothing.

[[Saya]] does not know the sessions were meant for her. She knows something terrible happened. She has never asked for details. Kael has never offered them.

→ See [The Volunteer](The%20Volunteer.md) for the full weight of this secret.

### The Void Connection — Cracked Open

The experiments did not break Kael's connection to [The Between](The%20Between.md). They _cracked it open_.

What emerged frightens even her in quiet moments. She no longer simply channels the Void — she _hears_ it. The membrane between this world and [The Between](The%20Between.md) grew thin in the places where she was hurt and never fully healed. Transference comes to her like breathing. The [Warframes](The%20Warframes.md) she connects with do not just respond — they _recognize_ her.

She does not speak of this.

### After the Fall of the Orokin

When [The Orokin](/The%20Orokin.md) collapsed, Kael moved through the ruins with purpose. She freed Warframes from abandoned facilities and helped build what would become [Velanthas](Velanthas.md) — the relay the sisters made into a home for the surviving Vael near the ruins of [Vaelos](Vaelos.md).

She has not stopped moving since.

## Personality

Kael speaks simply and directly. She does not over-explain. She makes decisions quickly and carries them quietly. She is not cold — those who know her well know there is enormous warmth underneath — but she has learned to keep most of herself behind the work.

She is fiercely, completely devoted to [[Saya]]. This is not weakness. It is, in its way, the most powerful thing about her — the absolute clarity of knowing what she would give and what she would not let be taken.

She is learning, slowly, to exist outside the war. It does not come naturally yet.

---

## The Warframes

Kael does not see the [Warframes](The%20Warframes.md) as weapons. She sees them as kindred — bodies shaped by Orokin hands for Orokin purposes, carrying inside them something that was never the Orokin's to own.

She is careful with them. She does not force connection. When she pilots frames of particular ferocity — Garuda, Valkyr, Voruna — she does not suppress what rises. She lets it move through her and comes home after.

Her collection has its own room in the royal section of [Velanthas](Velanthas.md). She maintains them herself.

→ See [The Gold Anchor](The%20Gold%20Anchor.md) for what the gold trim means in those moments.

## Relationships

- **[[Saya]]** — Her younger sister. The reason for everything. The one person she will never fully let see the cost.
- **[[Sorin]]** — Her royal guard. She knows him as duty, as a fixed presence at the edge of her rooms. She knows he carries something hidden and has chosen not to push it — the Between reads no malice in him, and that is enough.
- **[The Vael](/The%20Vael%20People.md)** — Her people. She protects them through [[Saya]].
- **[The Warframes](The%20Warframes.md)** — Kindred. Fellow survivors of Orokin cruelty.
- **[The Orokin](/The%20Orokin.md)** — Gone. She is indifferent to the ruins. She is not indifferent to what still crawls out of them.

---

## Vaelos

Kael has not returned to [Vaelos](Vaelos.md) since the fall. The planet sits close enough to [Velanthas](Velanthas.md) that its absence is a constant presence — known, felt, not yet faced.

A return is being planned.

She does not speak about what she expects to find.
