## Overview

Saya is the younger of the two sisters. She did not ask for the throne, did not scheme toward it, did not campaign for it. Her older sister [[Kael]] turned to her one day with the expression she wears when a decision has already been made, and said: _you should be the one._

Saya had learned, by then, that arguing with [[Kael]] about decisions already made was a specific kind of futile.

She accepted. She has spent every day since trying to be worthy of what that acceptance cost her sister — even though she only knows part of what it cost.

---

## History

### Before the Fall

Saya grew up on [Vaelos](../Locations/Vaelos.md) three years younger than [[Kael]] and thoroughly, contentedly in her older sister's orbit. Her own connection to [The Between](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) was real and present but quieter than [[Kael]]'s — more like candlelight than a deep river. She was studious, observant, and deeply attached to her sister in the way of someone who has always felt safest in one particular person's presence.

### The Fall of Vaelos

Saya was sixteen when [The Orokin](../Factions/The%20Orokin.md) came. She survived because [[Kael]] made sure she survived. She was captured along with her sister and a small number of other [Vael](../Factions/The%20Vael%20People.md).

→ See [Vaelos](../Locations/Vaelos.md) for the full account.

### The Experiments — What She Knows and Doesn't

The [The Orokin](../Factions/The%20Orokin.md) selected Saya for Void experimentation. She never went.

[[Kael]] volunteered to take her place before she could be taken.

Saya was not told this. She was told something vague about the selection being changed, an administrative decision, nothing to worry about. She was young enough and frightened enough that she accepted the explanation, at first.

As the sessions continued and [[Kael]] came back each time with new damage — burns, then scars, then silences, then something altered behind her eyes that never fully left — Saya understood that something terrible was happening. She did not know the specifics. She has never asked.

There is a mercy in not asking questions whose answers change nothing except how much you grieve.

What she does not know — and what [[Kael]] has decided she will never know — is that every mark her sister carries was meant for her.

→ See [The Volunteer](../Concepts/The%20Volunteer.md)

### After the Fall of the Orokin

When [The Orokin](../Factions/The%20Orokin.md) collapsed, Saya took leadership of the surviving [Vael](../Factions/The%20Vael%20People.md) — not because she wanted power but because [[Kael]] asked her to, and because someone had to, and because refusing would have made everything [[Kael]] had endured meaningless.

She rules with patience and precision — qualities she built deliberately because the weight she carries requires steadiness she doesn't always feel.

## Personality

Saya is not cold but she is _controlled_ — there is a difference, and those close to her know it. She thinks carefully before she speaks. She considers consequences. She carries guilt quietly and thoroughly, including the guilt of what she suspects happened to [[Kael]] and cannot bring herself to confirm.

She loves her older sister with the particular ache of someone who knows they are loved in return at tremendous cost and cannot find a way to make that cost smaller.

She has never once looked at [[Kael]] in her most frightening forms — Garuda, Valkyr, Voruna, the frames that carry ferocity and hunger and rage — and seen anything other than her sister. This is not naivety. It is a choice she makes every time, deliberately, because she knows what it means to [[Kael]] to be seen clearly and loved anyway.

---

## The Gold

Saya wears Orokin gold — not as tribute to those who destroyed her people, but as _reclamation_. The aesthetic of their oppressors repurposed as the aesthetic of survival.

_We are still here. We took what you built and made it ours._

The gold was also, in a way, given to her by [[Kael]] — who handed her the throne and everything that came with it. Every time Saya looks at the gold she wears she thinks of her sister.

## Relationships

- **[[Kael]]** — Her older sister. The person she is safest with. The person she worries about most. The person she will never fully stop feeling she owes.
- **[The Vael](../Factions/The%20Vael%20People.md)** — Her people. She leads them in [[Kael]]'s name and her own.
- **[The Warframes](../Concepts/The%20Warframes.md)** — She leaves this to [[Kael]]. Entirely.
- **[The Orokin](../Factions/The%20Orokin.md)** — Gone. The ruins are hers to rule over now. She finds no satisfaction in this.

## The Question That Haunts Her

Saya knows something was done to [[Kael]] in those rooms. She has pieced together enough from the scars and the silences to know it was not small and it was not accidental and it lasted a long time.

She does not know it was meant for her.

Sometimes she looks at [[Kael]] across a room and feels a grief she cannot name for something she cannot identify, and she thinks: _there is something I am not being told._

She has decided not to pull that thread.

[[Kael]] has decided the same.

Between them, the truth lives in the silence they have both, separately, agreed to keep.
