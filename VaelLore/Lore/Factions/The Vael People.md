## Overview

The Vael were a civilization that existed quietly on [Vaelos](../Locations/Vaelos.md) — whole, self-sufficient, and entirely unknown to the wider system until [The Orokin](The%20Orokin.md) followed the resonance of [Void energy](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) to their doorstep.

They were not a warlike people. They were not hiding. They were simply _there_, living in the way of a civilization that had found a sustainable relationship with what lived inside them and had stopped asking whether it was something to fear.

---

## Void Connection — _The Between_

Every Vael was born with some degree of connection to what they called [The Between](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) — their name for what the rest of the system calls the Void. This was not a rare mutation or a dangerous anomaly. It was a natural condition of their biology, built over generations of living near a stable Void rift their ancestors had long ago stopped fearing.

They understood [The Between](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) not as a weapon or a resource but as a _presence_ — the sense that the space between things was not empty but listening. Different Vael had different depths of connection and different ways of expressing it:

- **Philosophers** who debated its nature
- **Healers** who used it with precision and gentleness
- **Warriors** who channelled it with intensity
- **Artists** who shaped light and perception through it

They did not divide these expressions into acceptable and forbidden. They did not build walls around [The Between](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) and call those walls wisdom. To be Vael was to understand that the full current of what lived inside you — including the parts that were dark and vast and difficult — was not corruption. It was honesty.

---

## Society

The Vael were not uniformly peaceful — no people are — but they were _whole_. Their society was built around the understanding that [The Between](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) was a shared inheritance, not a hierarchy of power. Leadership carried responsibility rather than privilege. The strongest connection to the Void did not automatically confer authority — it conferred _duty_.

This is the tradition that made [[Kael]] the rightful heir of her family's line. And it is the tradition she honoured, in her own way, by choosing her sister over the throne.

## What Might Have Been

The Vael would have found the Tenno eventually. The Void connects what it touches, and two peoples touched by it would have drifted toward each other across the stars like two currents in the same sea.

What they would have built together — what understanding might have passed between a civilization that had always lived with [The Between](../Concepts/The%20Between.md) and children who had been transformed by sudden violent exposure to it — is a question that will never be answered.

The [Orokin](The%20Orokin.md) found the Tenno first, and then found the Vael, and made sure that question could never be asked.

---

## The Fall

When [The Orokin](The%20Orokin.md) discovered the Vael, they did not respond with diplomacy or wonder. A civilization of naturally Void-touched people was not a miracle to them. It was a variable they could not control — a power they had not granted and therefore could not own.

The war was brief. [Orokin](The%20Orokin.md) forces came with Warframes and Void weaponry and the cruelty of those who believe their survival justifies any cost paid by others. [Vaelos](../Locations/Vaelos.md) burned.

Of an entire civilization, only a small number survived.

→ See [Vaelos](../Locations/Vaelos.md) for the full account of the fall.

---

## What Remains

The surviving Vael are scattered. Some were found by [[Kael]] in the aftermath of the Orokin's fall and brought under [[Saya]]'s protection. Some are still lost somewhere in the system. Some are gone in ways that cannot be undone.

Their culture survives in fragments:

- The old word for the Void — _the Between_ — still used by [[Kael]] in private moments
- The small rituals [[Saya]] maintains that have no practical purpose except that their mother maintained them too
- The understanding, shared between the sisters without needing to be spoken, that the dark current inside them is not a flaw

It is simply what they are. It always was.
