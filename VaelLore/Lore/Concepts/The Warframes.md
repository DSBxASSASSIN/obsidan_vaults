## Overview

[[Kael]]'s relationship to the Warframes is one of the most defining aspects of who she is — and one of the most private. She does not speak about it often. She does not need to. It shows in how she moves in them, how she treats them, how she came to have the ones she has.

---

## Kindred, Not Tools

The [[../Factions/The Orokin.md|Orokin]] built the Warframes as weapons — bodies shaped and repurposed for Orokin use, carrying inside them something that was never the Orokin's to own. [[Kael]] understands this in a way that is not purely intellectual.

She was also a body shaped by Orokin hands for Orokin purposes. She also carries inside her something they tried to extract and use and could never fully own. The parallel is not lost on her. She has never said it aloud.

When she looks at a Warframe she does not see a weapon or a tool or a vehicle. She sees a kindred — something that was taken, shaped against its nature, made to serve, and survived anyway.

---

## How She Came to Have Them

[[../Factions/The Orokin.md|[Kael]]'s fall.

She did not take them. She _freed_ them.

The distinction matters to her, even if no one else would notice the difference.

## Transference

[[The Between.md|[Kael]] — cracked open and deepened by the Orokin experiments — makes Transference feel different for her than it presumably does for the Tenno. She does not just _pilot_ a frame. She connects with it. The frames she operates do not merely respond to her — they _recognize_ her, in the way of things that have been through similar things and know the shape of it in each other.

She is careful with this. She does not rush connection. She does not force it. She meets each frame where it is.

## Notable Frames

### Voruna Prime

_The war beast. The pack._

Voruna Prime is perhaps the frame [[Kael]] has the deepest kinship with — the wolf who fights for the pack, the predator who is also a protector. The instinct that lives in Voruna is not simply violence. It is loyalty made feral.

[[Kael]] understands this completely.

Voruna Prime wears black armor with deep crimson underlighting and gold trim — [[Kael]]'s full color language expressed in the frame's aesthetic. When she operates Voruna, the gold anchor is present. Voruna is one of the frames that needs it most.

### Garuda / Garuda Prime

_The hunger. The precision._

Garuda is ferocity as art — the frame that does not rage but _carves_, deliberately, with something adjacent to pleasure. [[Kael]] does not suppress what rises in her in Garuda. She lets it move. The gold anchor matters here.

### Valkyr / Valkyr Prime

_The rage. The compressed grief._

Valkyr carries within her frame a particular kind of fury — the kind built from being taken and shaped and remade against one's nature. [[Kael]] does not need to imagine what that feels like. She knows. She is gentle with Valkyr in the quiet moments between deployments. She does not think anyone would understand why.

### Follie

_The jester. The chaos._

Follie is the frame most unlike [[Kael]]'s nature — wild, unpredictable, draped in organic excess. [[Kael]] wears her in dark crimson and black, the fleshy tendrils the colour of dried blood, the gold anchor present in subtle trim.

She is harder to read in Follie than in most frames. [[Saya]] finds this unsettling in a way she has not articulated.

---

## [[Saya]] and the Warframes

[[Saya]] does not pilot Warframes. This is not a limitation — it is a choice, and it is [[Kael]]'s choice as much as her own.

[[Kael]] does not want her sister on the battlefield. This is the entire point. [[Saya]] leads. [[Kael]] fights. The Warframes are part of how [[Kael]] makes sure that arrangement holds.

[[Saya]] has never pushed back on this. She understands, without it being said, that the frames are [[Kael]]'s domain — and that taking that domain from her would be a kind of harm masquerading as inclusion.

She watches sometimes, from whatever safety [[Kael]] has arranged. She does not look away.
