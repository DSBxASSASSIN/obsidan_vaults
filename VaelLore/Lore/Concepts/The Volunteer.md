## Overview

The Volunteer is the name for the secret at the heart of everything between [[../Factions/The Orokin.md|[Kael]] experiments, and has never told her.

It is not a page about a heroic act. It is a page about what that act costs, quietly, every day.

---

## What Happened

When the [[../Factions/The Orokin.md|Orokin]] captured the surviving [[../Factions/The Vael People.md|Vael]] following the fall of [[../Locations/Vaelos.md]], their researchers selected subjects for Void experimentation — attempts to understand and extract the [[The Between.md|Vael's natural Void connection]] for Orokin use.

[[Saya]] was selected first.

[[Kael]] volunteered to take her place before her sister could be taken.

She did not beg. She did not negotiate. She stated it — clearly, simply, in the way of someone for whom the decision had already been made in a place deeper than words. The Orokin researchers found a willing subject more useful than an unwilling one. They accepted.

---

## The Sessions

[[Kael]] came back from the first session with burns along her forearms that she wrapped before [[Saya]] could see.

She came back from the fifth with a new scar along her jaw and a silence that lasted three days.

She came back from the sessions that followed with less she was willing to show, and something behind her eyes that had not been there before and has never fully left — a depth of connection to [[The Between.md]] that went beyond anything her training had produced, as if the Void itself had noticed what was being done to her and moved closer.

She told [[Saya]] nothing specific. An administrative change in the selection. Nothing to worry about. [[Saya]] was young and frightened and accepted it, at first. As the damage accumulated and the silences lengthened, she stopped believing the explanation and started believing something worse — that something terrible was happening to her sister, that it had been happening for a long time, that there was nothing she could do about it.

She was right about all of that. She was wrong about one thing: she did not know that every mark was meant for her.

---

## What Saya Knows

[[Saya]] knows:

- Something terrible happened to [[Kael]] in the Orokin facilities
- It happened repeatedly, over time, and each time she came back worse
- [[Kael]] has never spoken about it directly
- Whatever it was, it changed her sister in ways that are still visible

[[Saya]] does not know:

- That she was the original selection
- That [[Kael]] chose this
- That every scar her sister carries was meant for her

She carries guilt for what she suspects. She would be destroyed by the truth. [[Kael]] knows this. That is why the truth has never been spoken and will never be spoken.

---

## What Kael Carries

[[Kael]] does not carry this as resentment. That is important.

She made the choice freely, in the full understanding of what she was choosing. She would make it again. She does not want [[Saya]] to know because she does not want her sister to live under the weight of it — not because she is angry, but because protecting [[Saya]] from that weight is itself an extension of the same love that made her volunteer in the first place.

She carries the physical marks. She carries the altered connection to [[The Between.md]] — cracked open in ways that sometimes frighten her. She carries the knowledge of what was done to her in those rooms, which she has never shared with anyone.

None of this, in her accounting, is a burden. It is simply the cost of the thing she chose. She paid it. [[Saya]] is safe. The accounting is complete.

This is either the healthiest possible relationship to sacrifice or a form of self-erasure so deep it has become invisible to the person doing it. Possibly both.

---

## The Silence Between Them

Both sisters have independently decided not to pull the thread.

[[Saya]] has decided not to ask the questions whose answers would change nothing except how much she had to grieve.

[[Kael]] has decided that [[Saya]] will never be told.

Between them, the truth lives in the silence they have both, separately, agreed to keep. They have never discussed this agreement. They do not need to. They have known each other long enough that some things are understood without words.

This silence is not dishonesty. It is, in its own way, an act of love from both sides — [[Kael]] choosing not to burden her sister, [[Saya]] choosing not to force open something her sister has chosen to keep closed.

Whether it is sustainable is a different question.

---

## The Open Question

_Will Saya ever find out?_

This is unresolved. It may always be unresolved. Some possibilities:

- She never finds out. The silence holds forever. She carries the guilt of what she suspects to the end.
- She finds out through external means — Orokin records, a third party, something [[Kael]] says in an unguarded moment.
- [[Kael]] tells her. Voluntarily. At a moment when the cost of silence finally outweighs the cost of truth.
- [[Saya]] asks directly. And [[Kael]], for once, does not protect her from the answer.

What happens after is its own story.
