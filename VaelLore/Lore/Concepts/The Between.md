
> _"The Orokin called it the Void. A resource. A place. Something outside of things. The Vael called it the Between — because it was never outside of anything. It was the space inside everything, listening."_

---

## Overview

_The Between_ is the [Vael](../Factions/The%20Vael%20People.md) name for what the rest of the Origin System calls the Void. The difference in naming is not merely linguistic — it reflects a fundamentally different understanding of what the Void is and what it means to carry it inside you.

---

## The Vael Understanding

To the [Vael](../Factions/The%20Vael%20People.md), the Between was not a place you went or a power you wielded. It was a _presence_ — the sense that the space between things was not empty but aware. Not conscious in the way of a person, but _listening_ in the way of deep water or old stone.

It ran through everything on [Vaelos](../Locations/Vaelos.md) because of the ancient Void rift near which their civilization had built itself over generations. Their biology had incorporated it across that time until it was simply part of what they were — expressed differently in different people, present in all of them.

They did not divide expressions of the Between into acceptable and dangerous, light and dark. They understood it as a full current — deep and sometimes violent and sometimes gentle and always, underneath everything, connected.

---

## Kael's Connection

[[Kael]]'s connection to the Between was always deep — more river than stream, more current than trickle. Her teachers spoke of it quietly among themselves.

What the [Orokin](../Factions/The%20Orokin.md) experiments did was crack it open.

The membrane between [[Kael]] and the Between grew thin in the places where she was hurt during the experiments and never fully healed. What came through is something that unsettles even her — a depth of awareness that goes beyond technique or training. She does not simply channel the Between. She _hears_ it. She feels the shape of space around her differently than she used to. The [Warframes](The%20Warframes.md) she connects with through Transference do not just respond to her — they _recognize_ her, the way kindred things recognize each other.

She does not speak of this.

## Saya's Connection

[[Saya]]'s connection to the Between is quieter — more candlelight than deep river. It is always _present_ but it does not pull at her the way it pulls at [[Kael]]. It shows most visibly in her eyes, which burn a constant deep red — the Between made visible, never hidden, never suppressed.

She has never tried to hide it. It is simply part of what she is.

→ See [[Saya]]

## The Orokin's Understanding

The [Orokin](../Factions/The%20Orokin.md) understood the Void as a resource — something outside of normal space that could be accessed, harvested, and weaponized. They built their entire civilization's highest achievements around this understanding.

What they could never grasp — what their experiments on [[Kael]] failed to extract — was the Vael understanding that the Between was not outside of things. It was _inside_ them. You could not isolate it from the person who carried it any more than you could isolate a river from the valley it carved.

Whatever they thought they learned, they learned it wrong.
