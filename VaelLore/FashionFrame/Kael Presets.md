# Kael — Drifter Presets

> _"The enemy sees a monster. Her sister sees her. That difference is everything."_

---

## Overview

[[Kael.md]]'s three Drifter presets are not separate characters — they are the same person dressed for different kinds of work. The black and crimson run through all three. The gold accent trim runs through all three. The meaning of that gold never changes.

→ See [[../Lore/Concepts/The Gold Anchor.md]] for what the gold means. → See [[../Lore/Concepts/The Warframes.md]] for her relationship to the frames she pilots.

## Color Philosophy

| Channel       | Color                                 | Meaning                                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Primary**   | Deep black                            | The shadow she chose to live in                              |
| **Secondary** | Dark crimson — dried blood, not fresh | Something that has already paid its cost                     |
| **Tertiary**  | Deep black                            |                                                              |
| **Accents**   | Subtle warm gold                      | [[../Lore/Concepts/The Gold Anchor.md|The anchor]] — _I come home_  |
| **Emissive**  | Deep pure crimson                     | [[../Lore/Concepts/The Between.md]] moving through her, visible when she lets it |
| **Energy**    | Deep pure crimson — unified, no white | The Void. Clean. Undiluted.                                  |

**On the energy color:** There should be no white or grey mixed into the energy channel. The Void that moves through [[Kael.md]] is not pale or washed out. It is the color of something old and deep and entirely serious.

## Preset A — The Operative

_"The dangerous stranger you don't look at twice. Until it's too late."_

### Appearance

- Sleek black tactical coat, layered
- **Vayas Prime Mask** — half-face coverage, black and crimson detail
- **Revenant Mephisto Diadem** — dark horns, the only thing that marks her as something other than human at a glance
- Hair down, black with deep crimson underlights — red visible only in certain light
- Long dark cape/coat flowing behind her

### Mood

Controlled. Deliberate. This is [[Kael.md]] choosing exactly how much of herself to show — which is: very little. She moves through the world in Preset A like someone who has already calculated every exit in the room and decided none of them will be necessary.

This is her default. The version of herself she presents when the work requires precision over power.

### When She Wears It

Infiltration. Diplomacy that might become violence. Situations where being underestimated is useful. Any context where the goal is to be in and out before anyone fully registers what happened.

---

## Preset B — The Warlord

_"When she stops being careful about what she shows."_

### Appearance

- Heavy dark armor, substantial and imposing
- Deep crimson underlighting — the Void burning visibly beneath the surface
- Full masked silhouette — nothing of her face visible
- The silhouette alone is a statement

### Mood

This is [[Kael.md]] when something has threatened [[Saya.md]] and subtlety has become a luxury she is no longer willing to afford. There is no performance here, no calculation of how she appears. There is only the work and the willingness to do it completely.

She is not angrier in Preset B. She is _clearer_. The restraint she carries in Preset A is not gone — it has simply become irrelevant to the current situation.

### When She Wears It

When the cost doesn't matter, only the outcome. When something needs to end and she is the one who will end it. When [[Saya.md]] needs to not see what happens next.

---

## Preset C — Off Duty

_"She exists outside the war, sometimes. She is learning how."_

### Appearance

- Layered dark coat — less armored, more worn
- Gold-trimmed gas mask — different aesthetic, more worn-in
- Hair half-up
- Black star/diamond face paint under one eye
- Slightly more relaxed silhouette overall

### Mood

[[Kael.md|kael]] does not have many off-duty moments. This preset exists because [[Saya.md]] asked her once, carefully, if she ever just _existed_ somewhere without it being about protection or combat or the next thing that needed doing.

Preset C is the answer. It is imperfect and a little awkward because existing outside the war does not come naturally to her yet. But she is trying.

The star face paint was [[Saya.md]]'s idea.

### When She Wears It

Rare moments of actual rest. When she is with [[Saya.md]] somewhere that is not a battlefield. When she is trying to remember what it felt like to just be a person.

## The Gold Across All Three

Every preset carries the gold accent trim. It is never dominant — always subtle, always secondary to the black and crimson. It does not announce itself.

It does not need to.

[[Kael.md|kael]] knows where it is. That is enough.

## The Dangerous Frames — Gold as Anchor

When [[Kael.md|kael]] pilots frames of particular ferocity, the gold accent trim takes on its deepest meaning. These are the frames where the connection to [[../Lore/Concepts/The Between.md]] pulls hardest, where the line between her and what she is channelling becomes something she has to hold consciously.

|Frame|What rises in her|
|---|---|
|**Garuda**|Hunger. The pleasure of precision violence.|
|**Valkyr**|Rage. Pure and structural and very old.|
|**Voruna**|Predator instinct. The pack logic that has no patience for mercy.|

She does not suppress these things. She lets them move. She comes home after.

The gold says: _[[Saya.md]] once looked at me in this form and saw only me. I am still that person. I come home._
