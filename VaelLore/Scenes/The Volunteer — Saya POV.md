# Scene: The Volunteer — Saya POV

Occurs during [The Volunteer](../Lore/Concepts/The%20Volunteer.md) involving:

- [The Orokin](../Lore/Factions/The%20Orokin.md)

---

## Scene

They didn’t say her name twice.

They didn’t need to.

Saya stepped forward before she realized she had moved.

The room felt smaller than it had a moment ago.

Like something had already closed around her.

She could feel Kael beside her.

Still.

Too still.

“Wait.”

The word came out sharper than she expected.

The Orokin didn’t respond.

They weren’t looking at her anymore.

They were already treating it as decided.

Saya turned—

And Kael spoke.

“Take me instead.”

No hesitation.

No tension.

Like it had already been decided somewhere else.

“That’s not how this works,” Saya said.

But even as she said it, she knew—

It didn’t matter.

Kael was already moving.

Already stepping forward.

Already taking her place.

“You don’t get to do that,” Saya said, stepping after her.

Kael looked back.

Just once.

Calm.

Steady.

Familiar.

“It’s already done.”

Saya stopped.

Not because she agreed.

Because something in Kael’s expression told her—

This wasn’t the moment the decision was made.

She was just seeing it happen.

Too late to change it.

“Kael—”

But she didn’t finish.

Because Kael had already turned away.

Already stepped forward.

Already gone.

And the Orokin—

Accepted it.

Without question.

Like it didn’t matter which one of them it was.

That was the part that stayed with her.

Not the choice.

Not the fear.

The fact that it didn’t matter.

Saya stood there long after they were gone.

Trying to understand when she had lost the ability to stop it.

And realizing—

She never had it.
