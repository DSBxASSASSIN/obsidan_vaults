## Scene

They did not restrain her immediately.

That surprised her.

She had expected force.

Resistance.

Something she could push against.

Instead—

They observed.

Measured.

Spoke to each other in quiet, clinical tones as if she were already something else.

Kael didn’t speak.

Didn’t ask questions.

There was no point.

She already knew why she was here.

“Begin.”

That was the only word that mattered.

The first sensation wasn’t pain.

It was pressure.

Not on her body.

Inside it.

Like something was being pressed open from the wrong side.

Her breath caught—

Not from fear.

From disorientation.

This wasn’t damage.

Not yet.

This was access.

They weren’t trying to break her.

They were trying to reach something.

The Between responded.

That was the worst part.

It didn’t resist.

It didn’t recoil.

It answered.

Kael felt it shift—

Not away from her.

Toward her.

Something in her connection stretched.

Thinned.

Opened.

Now the pain came.

Not sharp.

Not immediate.

Gradual.

Precise.

Deliberate.

Like someone learning exactly how far they could go without losing the subject.

She didn’t scream.

That surprised them.

She could tell.

Not from what they said.

From how long they let it continue.

They were waiting for a reaction.

They didn’t get one.

What they got instead—

Was deeper access.

That was when she understood.

This wouldn’t be one session.

This was a process.

And she had just made it easier.
