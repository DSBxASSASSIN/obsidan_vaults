# Scene: First Contact

## Context

Occurs after the fall of [The Orokin](../Lore/Factions/The%20Orokin.md), during Kael’s initial recovery phase while searching abandoned facilities.

---

## Scene

The facility still had power.

Not fully.

Enough.

Lights flickered in long, uneven intervals, leaving the corridor in alternating bands of gold and shadow.

Kael didn’t follow the light.

She followed the silence.

There was something wrong with it.

Not empty.

Waiting.

She stopped outside a sealed chamber.

The door had been forced open long ago—something had tried to leave.

Or something had tried to get in.

She didn’t touch the controls.

Didn’t need to.

She closed her eyes instead.

And listened.

Not for sound.

For pressure.

For that familiar sense of something just beyond the edge of perception.

There.

Faint.

But present.

She stepped inside.

The Warframe stood at the far end of the room.

Still.

Head lowered.

Motionless in the way only something _not truly asleep_ can be.

Kael didn’t move closer immediately.

She let the space settle.

Let whatever was left inside it adjust to her being there.

A long moment passed.

Then—

It shifted.

Not fully.

Not a step.

Just enough.

A slight adjustment of weight.

A recognition of presence.

Kael exhaled slowly.

“You’re not empty,” she said.

The Warframe did not respond.

But it did not return to stillness either.

That was enough.

She stepped forward.

Careful.

Measured.

No sudden movement.

No attempt to force anything.

The distance closed.

The air changed.

The pressure deepened.

For a moment—just a moment—she felt something push back.

Not resistance.

Awareness.

Then she reached for it.

Not like the Orokin would have.

Not cutting.

Not forcing.

Opening.

The connection took.

Fast.

Too fast.

Like something had been waiting for it.

The Warframe lifted its head fully this time.

And moved.

Not as something controlled.

But as something that had finally been allowed to finish a motion it had started a long time ago.

Kael did not flinch.

She only steadied herself inside it.

And let it recognize her.
