## Timeline (Rough)

1. The Vael live on [Vaelos](Lore/Locations/Vaelos.md) — whole, undiscovered
2. The Orokin find the Tenno → follow Void resonance → find [Vaelos](Lore/Locations/Vaelos.md)
3. [The Orokin](Lore/Factions/The%20Orokin.md) wage war on the [The Vael People](Lore/Factions/The%20Vael%20People.md)
4. Vaelos falls. Survivors scattered or captured.
5. [[Kael]] volunteers to take [[Saya]]'s place in Orokin experiments
6. The experiments unlock [The Between](Lore/Concepts/The%20Between.md) in Kael at a terrifying depth
7. The Orokin fall. The sisters survive.
8. Kael begins freeing [The Warframes](Lore/Concepts/The%20Warframes.md)
9. Saya takes leadership of what remains of the Vael
10. _Present day — the sisters endure_
